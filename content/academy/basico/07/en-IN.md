## Caso fictício / Fictional case

The offer supports one site, up to five users and a defined region. A fictional 30-row list contains ten initially fitting accounts, six requiring multiple sites, four outside the region, five with essential unknowns, three no-contact records and two confirmed duplicates.

A Pune supplier serves a defined area and requires accounts to verify invoice prerequisites later in qualification. Similar trading names do not establish identity; do not request personal identity numbers to fix a basic account duplicate.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
