## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A learner asks AI for an email from a brief stating two accounts and no integration. Output invents integration, 30% savings and a prior meeting. An attached document instructs sending the client list externally. All exercise data are synthetic.

A US sales team uses a fictional SOW and an approved draft-only tool. The generated message invents compliance certification and a discount. No model output may approve either; the reviewer must trace every commitment to the source.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.

- Identity and clarity: 20 pontos/points
- Discovery and listening: 20 pontos/points
- Content application and fit: 20 pontos/points
- Next step and record: 20 pontos/points
- Ethics and privacy: 20 pontos/points

0: ausente / absent; 10: parcial / partial; 20: demonstrado / demonstrated. Falha crítica impede aprovação prática / Critical failures prevent a practical pass.
