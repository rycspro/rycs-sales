Clear Line offers work-order organisation. The contact has one minute, uses an incumbent and has not measured delays. The exercise list is approved, but there is no permission to record real calls. After two questions the contact asks not to be called again.

## Projeto / Project

Write a 30-second opening, two questions and three endings: busy, requested material and final refusal. Perform the refusal branch without overriding the restriction.

**Resposta-modelo / Model answer**
Identify company and purpose; check relevance and the actual difficulty. Offer another time only if wanted. Send only requested material. On refusal, close and record the restriction without recording or calling from another number.
