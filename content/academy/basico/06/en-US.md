## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Paper Works posts order-organisation examples. A post received 200 views, 20 interactions and four application questions; only two people requested demos. The rep labelled all 20 opportunities and exported profiles. There is no permission to use client images.

A US craft-supply business attracts independent retailers through educational posts. One retailer asks about compatibility while another needs existing-customer support. Treat those conversations differently; public engagement is not a buying-project register.
