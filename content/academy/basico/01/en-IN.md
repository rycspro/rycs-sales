## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A fictional workshop needs to organise orders; verify the problem, funding and decision process.

## Module 1: What selling means: needs, solutions and value

### 1.1 — What selling means: needs, solutions and value

**Aula / Lesson**
Separate a feature, a possible benefit and buyer-confirmed value. A dashboard is a feature. Bringing orders together is a possible benefit. Whether that helps enough to justify cost and implementation depends on the buyer. The alternative may be an existing process rather than another vendor. Record current situation, confirmed difficulty, consequence and desired change. Mark an untested statement as a hypothesis. Do not estimate savings unless the inputs and assumptions are available, and do not present potential savings as guaranteed cash or income.

**Roteiro / Script**
Imagine you are speaking with Meera, who runs the fictional Lake Workshop in Pune. She says she needs an app. Do not assume the app is the problem to solve. Ask what her team needs to do and what gets in the way. Perhaps staff need to check order status without calling the front desk. That gives us a task to examine. A feature becomes useful only in a buyer’s circumstances; it does not guarantee business growth. Pause and write one question that could disprove your initial sales hypothesis.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. List two confirmed facts, two unknowns, a neutral discovery question and a conditional value hypothesis.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Facts: paper/messages and desire for remote status access. Unknowns: budget and migration effort. Ask how status is checked today. Hypothesis: a shared view may reduce the need to call the desk; validate that use.

The answer identifies what the buyer actually reported, keeps unmeasured impact separate and tests a conditional benefit rather than guaranteeing it.

### 1.2 — Products, services and integrated solutions

**Aula / Lesson**
An offer may combine a product, execution services and several coordinated components. Classify the components to clarify responsibilities, not to rank their sophistication. A measurable acceptance condition might require two test accounts to complete an agreed workflow. That checks delivery; it does not guarantee revenue, universal adoption or an error-free operation. Explain dependencies, recurring commitments and material exclusions before agreement. If pricing, tax treatment or a contract term is not provided, refer it to the authorised owner rather than inventing a rule.

**Roteiro / Script**
At Lake Workshop, Meera asks whether the team can learn the system without stopping service. A subscription alone does not answer that question. Separate access to the product from setup, training and migration. Ask what training arrangement is feasible, then check what the fictional offer actually includes. Do not promise language support or on-site work that has not been confirmed. On screen, show an inclusion, an exclusion and a buyer responsibility. Pause and explain the boundary in ordinary English, without hiding it in jargon.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Identify the components, explain one exclusion and write an acceptance condition for setup.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Product access plus setup/training services. Do not promise Marathi training or paper migration. Verify the two test accounts using the agreed workflow and confirm the training arrangement separately.

The acceptance test is limited to a deliverable that can be observed. It does not expand setup into migration, language support or a financial guarantee.

### 1.3 — B2B and B2C buying contexts

**Aula / Lesson**
B2B and B2C distinguish buying relationships, not fixed personalities. A business buyer can have emotional concerns; an individual can use a detailed comparison. Map users, technical or operational evaluators, economic approvers and contracting responsibilities. One person may hold several roles. Source S1 is a historical US retail estimate, not an India market statistic and not a conversion benchmark. Its published edition reported a 2024 online retail share of 16.1% and carries a later-revision notice. Check coverage and edition before drawing a conclusion.

**Roteiro / Script**
A purchase by a workshop and a purchase by a person for home use may involve the same device and different expectations. At Lake Workshop, Meera may approve the spend while the service supervisor checks whether staff can use the tool. Do not assume a job title reveals the whole decision process, or that a family-owned business has no formal approvals. Ask who will use, evaluate and authorise the purchase. If another language is preferred for a demonstration, confirm the arrangement instead of guessing from location or name.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Map three buying roles and write the question needed to identify the contracting process. Explain why a retail share does not predict a deal outcome.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Operational evaluator: supervisor; economic approver: Meera; contracting/invoice reviewers: unknown. Ask who must review the scope, invoice requirements and agreement. US retail share cannot predict this Indian workshop’s purchase.

Mapping use, evaluation and approval helps identify missing contracting responsibilities. An aggregate US retail measure cannot predict the outcome of a particular account.

### 1.4 — SDR, BDR, closer, salesperson and sales manager

**Aula / Lesson**
Specify responsibilities, output quality and handoff conditions. Initial qualification should produce reliable context, while negotiation must stay within delegated authority. Managers should review both activity and quality. Source S2 reports a 70% share of time on non-selling tasks in its surveyed sales-rep data. The report’s overall survey covered 5,500 sales professionals in 27 countries in March–April 2024; the overall sample is not necessarily the denominator for every chart. It is a vendor-published self-report, not a universal target or evidence that all administration is waste.

**Roteiro / Script**
A company may call a role business development executive, inside sales representative or SDR. The label alone does not define authority to quote, negotiate or commit to a timeline. In the Lake case, one colleague confirms the workflow and another leads the commercial discussion. The handoff must preserve what Meera actually said. It must also preserve restrictions, such as a request not to use a particular channel. Pause and describe the outcome of the role rather than the prestige of the title.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Rewrite the handoff and choose one quality measure with an explicit denominator.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Demo requested; purchase and terms not approved; confirm participants and purpose before the demo. Track accepted qualified handoffs divided by submitted handoffs, with the acceptance definition and period stated.

The revised handoff reports an observable request and preserves gaps. A quality measure needs both a defined outcome and the denominator against which it is reviewed.

## Module 2: Needs, preferences, difficulties and expectations

### 2.1 — Needs, preferences, difficulties and expectations

**Aula / Lesson**
A need concerns what must be addressed; a preference describes a desired form; a difficulty is an experienced obstacle; an expectation is a condition against which the buyer may judge delivery. These categories overlap and should help the conversation rather than diagnose the person. Ask for a recent example and an acceptable improvement. Not every impact must be monetised. If using a calculation, distinguish a hypothetical estimate from observed results and explain assumptions, implementation costs and what may not be recoverable as cash.

**Roteiro / Script**
Meera says she wants an app, but the underlying task is checking order status while away from the workshop. The preference for an app and the need for access are related but not identical. She also expects staff training that fits their working day. Ask which conditions are essential, which are preferences and what needs checking. Do not infer budget or willingness to change from the city, business size or language. Pause and write an observable success condition for the trial.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Identify one preference, one need and one expectation. Write a question for each and mark one condition you cannot promise.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Mobile-friendly form: preference. Remote status access: need to confirm. Agreed training arrangement: expectation. Ask who needs training and their preferred language; do not promise an unconfirmed training language.

The answer separates form preference from an operational need and an implementation expectation. It marks an unverified condition instead of promising an effortless change.

### 2.2 — Recognising a potential buyer profile

**Aula / Lesson**
An ideal customer profile describes account characteristics associated with potential fit. A buyer persona concerns a role and context in the decision. Neither proves purchase intent. Separate required fit conditions from tentative prioritisation signals and explicit exclusions. Record source, date and uncertainty. Unknown is a valid status. Privacy and outreach approval are separate checks from product fit; route legal questions to the responsible team. The India DPDP framework and relevant channel rules require review of applicability and effective provisions, not a claim that one generic script establishes compliance.

**Roteiro / Script**
Lake appears relevant because it has one location and three users, not because Meera belongs to a demographic group. The offer’s scope determines what we can support. Use sector, operational needs, service region and technical requirements. Do not collect Aadhaar, health details or family information for an initial sales discussion. If a field is not needed for this decision, leave it out. Public availability does not settle every data-protection question. Pause and specify the minimum fields your fictional account list needs.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Classify A–D, justify with product scope and contact restrictions, and name the minimum data needed.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
A: investigate need; B: outside scope; C: clarify user count through an approved process; D: no outreach. Keep only relevant account and process facts, with source/date and restrictions.

The classification uses stated scope and distinguishes unknown from incompatible. It also recognises that a matching account can still be excluded from contact.

### 2.3 — Open, closed and probing questions

**Aula / Lesson**
Use question types for different purposes. Open questions uncover context; probes develop a specific point; closed questions verify facts. A neutral sequence is context → recent example → consequence → confirmation. Questions about authority, resources or timing should help assess a path, not create an interrogation. Avoid leading phrasing, false alternatives and implied threats. If an answer is unavailable, record the gap. Do not ask for sensitive documents to make a basic qualification form appear complete.

**Roteiro / Script**
In a short Lake conversation, one clear question is often easier to answer than a long list of qualification fields. Ask how orders are recorded, then explore one example. Confirm details without converting “sometimes” into “every day”. If discussing budget, explain why the question matters and accept that the contact may need to check the process. Do not turn uncertainty into pressure. Pause and write an open question, a probe and a confirmation that still allow Meera to correct you.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Write one open question, one probe and one confirmation. Identify the unsupported inference.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
How do you check status today? Can you describe the last inconvenient check? Is remote access the task you want to test? Severe revenue loss was never confirmed.

The question sequence gathers context and a concrete episode, then invites correction. It leaves frequency and impact unconfirmed when the buyer has not measured them.

### 2.4 — Active listening and professional communication

**Aula / Lesson**
A faithful paraphrase preserves meaning and certainty. “May consider” does not mean “will purchase”. Confirm a summary and allow the buyer to correct it. Prefer observable notes to character judgements: “wants to verify compatibility” rather than “difficult buyer”. Do not score intelligence or interest from accent, response speed or cultural assumptions. Use only fictional data in role-play. Stop requests and privacy requests follow the organisation’s approved procedures; a rep should not promise deletion or a legal outcome without checking the applicable process.

**Roteiro / Script**
Professional English does not require copying a particular accent or speaking quickly. At Lake, check understanding and ask about preferred format when relevant. If Meera says she may review the idea later but does not want messages now, retain both facts: future interest is uncertain, and current outreach is restricted. Do not interpret a polite answer as agreement. Pause for the eight-minute practice: four minutes of conversation and four minutes of feedback based on the actual words exchanged.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Paraphrase faithfully, choose the immediate action and perform the formative role-play.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Future review is uncertain; the WhatsApp stop request is explicit. Stop the relevant commercial contact and record the restriction without switching channels to evade it.

A faithful summary retains both uncertain future interest and a definite present restriction. The chosen action respects the restriction instead of upgrading politeness into consent.

## Module 3: Prospecting: finding potential buyers

### 3.1 — Prospecting: finding potential buyers

**Aula / Lesson**
Keep an initial list small enough to inspect. Useful fields are account identifier, relevant fit facts, source/date, uncertainty, authorised channel and known restrictions. Remove duplicates and do not reuse data from another campaign without the approved basis and procedure. To review research quality, define a measure such as accounts meeting fit criteria divided by accounts reviewed during a stated period. That measure is about a specified sample and rule, not a guaranteed conversion rate for the entire market.

**Roteiro / Script**
For the Lake exercise, use invented accounts instead of scraping real businesses or copying a personal contact list. Research should produce a hypothesis with a source, not a claim that you know the buyer’s internal situation. Separate product fit from permission to act through a channel. A public number does not answer every operational or legal question. Pause and explain why an account that fits the product might still be excluded from an outreach sequence.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Choose an action for each account and write a prospecting objective that does not assume purchase intent.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Investigate the first workshop’s need, exclude the integration requirement, respect the opt-out. Objective: confirm use case and fit by an approved process, without assuming budget.

The plan excludes unsupported scope and prohibited outreach while retaining a legitimate investigation objective. It does not treat a public signal as proven purchase intent.

### 3.2 — Opening a conversation with a clear purpose

**Aula / Lesson**
A useful structure is identity → truthful context → relevant hypothesis → small request. Personalisation should explain relevance, not expose personal information. No reply is not a statement of interest and does not justify unlimited attempts. Do not use deceptive thread prefixes or invented prior relationships. Channel requirements are additional to clear writing. Use the organisation’s reviewed templates and preference process; do not treat one model sentence as proof that a campaign is legally compliant.

**Roteiro / Script**
For Lake, begin with the actual source: Meera submitted a request through a form. Introduce yourself and ask whether it is a good moment for two questions. Do not claim that a friend referred you unless that happened. If the contact prefers email rather than WhatsApp, confirm the preference rather than treating every channel as interchangeable. Active outreach needs an approved procedure under applicable rules. Pause and write separate openings for an inbound enquiry and a permitted active-contact scenario, showing the difference in context.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Write an inbound opening and explain which claim must be removed from an active-contact version.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Hello Meera, I’m Arun from Order Desk. Thank you for your website enquiry; I am replying by email as requested. May I ask how orders are recorded today? Do not claim an enquiry or referral for the other account.

The inbound version identifies the real source of interest. An active-contact version must remove nonexistent requests or referrals and requires its own approved channel process.

### 3.3 — Qualification: recognising a real opportunity

**Aula / Lesson**
Define qualification before counting outcomes. An operation may require a confirmed problem, basic fit, an appropriate participant and an accepted next step. Follow the actual rule rather than mechanically applying an acronym. Need does not imply priority; influence does not equal signing authority; interest does not prove available resources. Record the reason for the stage and the next authorised action. If there is no fit, say so and close or refer through an available process without fabricating urgency.

**Roteiro / Script**
Meera wants a demonstration but has not agreed to purchase. She says the supervisor should test the workflow. That is useful context, not a signed deal. Qualification examines need, operational fit, decision process, resources and timing as distinct questions. An owner’s title does not reveal whether another partner or process must approve the spend. Pause and ask about the approval path without making a personal financial judgement or treating an unknown budget as zero.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. List facts, unknowns and two qualification questions. Do not use hot/cold labels.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Facts: remote-status task and supervisor involvement. Unknowns: approval path and demo date. Ask who reviews investment and what test the supervisor would find useful.

Qualification separates confirmed need and interest from resources and timing. The next questions target the actual gaps rather than filling fields with convenient assumptions.

### 3.4 — Value presentations, negotiation and agreement

**Aula / Lesson**
Make a recommendation traceable: confirmed problem → relevant capability → available evidence → limitation → acceptance check. Compare offers on the same basis, including term, seats, dependencies and exclusions. Negotiation concerns authorised variables, not improvised commitments. A clear next step names action, responsible person, condition or date and buyer acceptance. Formal agreement requires the applicable authorisations and contracting process; compliments, proposal requests and demo attendance do not establish purchase on their own.

**Roteiro / Script**
At Lake, Meera needs to verify status access and training arrangements before considering the offer. Demonstrate those tasks and state what remains unconfirmed. If a quoted amount uses ₹, explain the billing period and whether taxes and services are specified; if they are not, ask the authorised team rather than inventing tax treatment. Do not convert interest into a confirmed order. Pause and write a responsible reply to a request for a discount outside your authority.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Reply to the discount request, identify one unresolved condition and write a next step.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
I can clarify the investment concern and consult the authorised team. Confirm the billing/tax basis and training scope before comparison; agree who will review the corrected quote and when.

The reply preserves pricing authority and identifies the unresolved scope or quote basis. A valid next step needs a responsible owner and an agreed condition, not an assumed purchase.

### 3.5 — After-sales care, relationships and referrals

**Aula / Lesson**
After-sales work verifies delivery, clarifies use within scope and tracks problems to an appropriate owner. Do not conceal a service issue to protect a sales metric or use a complaint to pressure an additional purchase. Check the buyer’s agreed success condition and record what actually happened. Testimonials, images and referrals need the relevant permissions and approved process. The course’s pass threshold is 16/20, which is 80%; this is a learning rule, not a market statistic. The quiz time is included in this lesson and must not be added again.

**Roteiro / Script**
At Lake, the supervisor has not yet completed the trial workflow. Do not claim a successful implementation just because Meera liked the demonstration. Confirm delivery and route difficulties through the agreed support process. An optional referral can use a public introduction the client chooses to share; avoid collecting third-party numbers as an obligation. Now take the twenty-question check, allowing twelve minutes within this lesson. The practical project remains part of the educational model even though the current platform does not enforce it.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Prioritise actions, draft an after-sales reply and complete the knowledge check.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Help through the agreed process without a referral condition. Confirm the actual trial result and record the issue. Referrals remain optional and do not determine support.

The response prioritises the existing delivery obligation and avoids claiming success while a material problem remains. Support must not depend on supplying new leads.

## Module 4: Reading RYCS campaigns and commercial opportunities

### 4.1 — Reading RYCS campaigns and commercial opportunities

**Aula / Lesson**
Prepare a reading sheet: objective, target account, activity, acceptance rule, permitted evidence, restrictions, query owner and stated commercial conditions. Distinguish execution deadlines, review timing and any payment-release conditions; do not invent values or dates. This material teaches interpretation rather than the current legal or payment rules of RYCS. Product owners must verify screens and workflows before recording. Do not use real contacts or perform a live campaign merely to complete the fictional exercise.

**Roteiro / Script**
If you use RYCS for an international opportunity, do not assume a Brazilian training example establishes eligibility, payout currency or local availability. Read the actual opportunity and terms. Our fictional exercise asks for a held meeting with a responsible workshop contact and an appropriate problem. It contains no payment amount. Evidence describes what happened; it does not make an unperformed activity valid. Pause and identify the missing operational and commercial conditions before deciding whether you could accept the task.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Fill in known conditions, list at least three gaps and explain why the example cannot support an earnings calculation.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Target/outcome are partly defined; eligibility, currency, payment conditions and evidence process need confirmation. The exercise does not establish India availability or income.

The reading sheet distinguishes the work requirement from missing evidence and commercial conditions. No payment calculation is supportable without the actual terms.

### 4.2 — Turning task instructions into acceptance checks

**Aula / Lesson**
Separate mandatory criteria, useful context and hypotheses. Quantity cannot compensate for a missing mandatory condition. A review matrix links requirement → permitted verification → source → status. A record supports only the event it actually establishes. If evidence is unavailable or collection is inappropriate, preserve the uncertainty and contact the responsible owner. Do not rewrite the assignment’s requirements inside your report to make the activity appear valid.

**Roteiro / Script**
Do not disqualify a workshop contact simply because the title sounds junior. Confirm the role required by the assignment. At the same time, do not assume an accepted invitation proves attendance or authority. A checklist should show the requirement, verification method and present status. If the evidence would require an unauthorised recording, do not collect it; clarify the approved route. Pause and identify what remains unknown when an assistant has accepted a meeting invite.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Build the requirement matrix and write one question about the contact’s role.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Scheduling acceptance confirmed; attendance, responsibility and need pending. Ask whether the coordinator owns the workflow or can include the person who evaluates it, without dismissing the title.

The matrix prevents a scheduling record from satisfying unrelated requirements. A participant’s title is not a substitute for confirming the operational role the task requires.

### 4.3 — Activity records and proportionate evidence

**Aula / Lesson**
Evidence needs authenticity, relevance, clarity and data minimisation. An invitation supports scheduling, not automatic attendance or payment approval. Before recording or sharing a conversation, check the applicable rules and approved procedure; this course grants no general recording permission. Preserve correction history through the available workflow. Record only what the event supports, maintain access restrictions and use the organisation’s incident process for accidental exposure rather than redistributing the material.

**Roteiro / Script**
A credible record is clear about both facts and gaps. For Lake, “requested a workflow demonstration; approval process not yet confirmed” is better than “deal closed”. Do not include Aadhaar numbers, personal health information or a full message archive to make a basic sales note look complete. Use authorised systems and access controls. Never upload real prospect records to a public repository or an unapproved AI tool. Pause and decide what must be left out of the fictional handoff.

**Exercício / Exercise**
1. Write a factual note and identify two unsupported claims or unnecessary data items to exclude.

**Resposta comentada / Answer guide**
Tuesday, 10:00 IST: trial outline requested for supervisor review; commercial approval and timing unknown. Exclude “confirmed order” and unnecessary personal identifiers.

The rewritten note distinguishes a request from a decision and removes certainty unsupported by the event. Minimisation excludes personal data that the task does not require.

**Aula / Lesson**
Read the stated review reason and use the available clarification or correction process. Never manufacture evidence. Compensation depends on actual opportunity conditions and valid work; training examples establish no entitlement or earnings promise. The proposed completion model requires all lessons, at least 16/20 on theory and at least 80/100 on the practical rubric without a critical ethical failure. Practical gating is not implemented in the existing platform. Keep the package marked for review until pedagogy, product, privacy, localisation and integration have been checked.

**Roteiro / Script**

If a meeting was postponed, record a postponed meeting rather than inventing completion. The final project uses six minutes of conversation and six of feedback. Assess clarity, discovery, fit, next step and ethics, not accent or speaking speed. A request to stop contact must be respected even if that means the conversation ends without a meeting.
