## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Atlas is asked to sign tomorrow while IT access review and the economic approver remain unavailable. The seller lacks discount authority and invented a Friday price doubling. No such term exists. The buyer asks to decide after technical review.

An Indian enterprise buyer needs regional operational review and central finance approval. The seller may arrange a review meeting but cannot infer approval from a sponsor's polite agreement to discuss.
