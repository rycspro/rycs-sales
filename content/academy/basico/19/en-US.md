## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A fictional dataset has 20 opportunities: eight won, four lost and eight still open. Of 30 activities, five duplicate existing records. Two opportunities end before their start date. A blank budget was converted to zero without confirmation.

A US CRM team imports timestamps from two time zones and must normalise display without changing event history. USD amount fields need term and scope; annual and monthly values are not comparable without conversion rules.
