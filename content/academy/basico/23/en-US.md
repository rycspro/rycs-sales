## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Three fictional ads await review: scheduling software promises to cure anxiety, a course guarantees passing every exam, and a digital product guarantees monthly income. A clinic offers patient records for a demo that can use synthetic data. Testimonials lack permission.

A US clinic-administration scenario needs synthetic scheduling records and responsible review of health implications, data handling and any applicable sector obligations. An educational seller must distinguish completion certificates from professional licensure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
