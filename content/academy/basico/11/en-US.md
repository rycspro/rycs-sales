## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Clear Form proposes analysis of five workflows and one report within four weeks after synthetic inputs are available. Implementation is excluded. A slide promises complete implementation in one week and guaranteed return. The buyer asks who reviews the report and what happens if inputs are missing.

A US professional-services buyer requests an SOW with acceptance criteria, named reviewers and separate implementation pricing. USD pricing, recurring terms and taxes must be confirmed rather than inferred from a slide.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
