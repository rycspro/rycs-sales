## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A team has an old name-and-phone list with no documented purpose. One contact requests no further outreach and access to their data. A seller exports it to a personal account and adds health information from a conversation. No commercial use is authorised.

An Indian scenario requires checking DPDP provisions and commencement, applicable telecom requirements and channel policy. Do not use a published rule as proof that every requirement has the same effective date.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
