## Caso fictício / Fictional case

North Services announced expansion. Clear Team offers sales-process organisation but does not know whether the expansion creates a need. Of 80 fictional eligible recipients, 12 replied: four refusals, five information requests and three meeting requests. One subject falsely says RE: our meeting.

A Chennai professional-services firm has announced a new office. Operations wants a workflow example; accounts needs billing details if a project advances. No buying timeline is confirmed and the preferred initial format is a short written note.
