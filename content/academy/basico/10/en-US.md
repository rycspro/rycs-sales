## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Bridge Services sent a requested proposal. The contact agreed follow-up after Friday's internal meeting, but automation scheduled daily messages. On Thursday the buyer asked for a pause until further notice. Another rep prepared a call. No genuine proposal expiry exists.

A Chicago buyer needs procurement feedback after a dated internal review. A calendar invitation must use America/Chicago for that date. Do not treat a seller's month-end forecast deadline as the buyer's deadline.
