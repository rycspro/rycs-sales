## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A supplier wants to sell one service to Ohio and Pune without confirmed local support, billing currency or tax treatment. An invitation says 10:00 without date or zone. The seller assumes English and WhatsApp for everyone. Buyers require different reviewers.

Build an Ohio scenario with an IT contractor, procurement review and America/New_York scheduling on an actual date. The buyer prefers email and needs a written scope. No inference about all US buyers follows from this case.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
