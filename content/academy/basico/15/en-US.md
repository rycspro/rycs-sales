## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A fictional service proposal costs 12,000 currency units for diagnosis and a plan, excluding implementation. The buyer offers 9,000 with implementation in ten days. The closer may request review but cannot approve discounts. Required inputs arrive in two weeks.

A US buyer evaluates USD 12,000 for a diagnostic SOW against USD 9,000 for a smaller review. Procurement and IT must check the scope; the executive sponsor cannot authorise unsupported implementation.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
