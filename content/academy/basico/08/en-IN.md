## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Clear Delivery reports ten repeated checks per week, each lasting 12 minutes, over four example weeks. Effort is valued at 40 currency units per hour, with no staffing reduction. Training is required and the legacy system is not integrated. The cause remains unconfirmed.

A Pune distributor values effort at a fictional INR 800/hour and requires supervisor review before training. Do not convert the US example as a market benchmark. The local process includes a second manual approval that may explain repeated checking.
