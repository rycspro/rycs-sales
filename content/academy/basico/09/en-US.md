## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A fictional proposal costs 1,200 currency units monthly for two service units with one onboarding session. The buyer compares it with 700 for one unit without onboarding, then reveals no change approval this quarter. No discount authority exists.

A US maintenance company compares USD 1,200/month for two teams with USD 700 for one. Finance requires a like-for-like annual scope comparison and operations is satisfied with the incumbent. Funding timing is unconfirmed.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
