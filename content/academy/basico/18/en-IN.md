## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A fictional team logged 100 scheduled meetings, 60 held and 30 meeting quality criteria within the same set. The manager rewards only scheduling and some records are duplicates. Four reps have different portfolios and complexity. Forecasts treat every proposal as guaranteed revenue.

An Indian manager oversees two regional teams with different sectors and language needs. Central accounts approval affects cycle time. Do not judge one team's capability from raw volume without adjusting the comparison and inspecting data quality.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.

- Discovery and listening: 20 pontos/points
