## Caso fictício / Fictional case

At Atlas, operations wants less rework, finance predictable cost and IT access control. The rep promised integration to IT but told operations no workflow change was needed. The offer exports files, not automatic integration. Implementation agreement is absent.

A US buyer includes an outside IT managed-service provider and a finance approver who has not attended the demo. The sponsor cannot waive security review or approve an annual commitment alone.

## Projeto / Project

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
