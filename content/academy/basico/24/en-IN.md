## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Process Studio offers analysis of four workflows and a report three weeks after inputs arrive, excluding implementation. The buyer adds training and integration without changing price or timing. Acceptance criteria and input ownership are missing. Sales promised an immediate start.

An Indian services buyer needs branch input owners and central accounts review. Training language and on-site availability are unresolved. INR proposal terms require separate costing rather than automatic conversion from a US example.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.

- Identity and clarity: 20 pontos/points
- Discovery and listening: 20 pontos/points
