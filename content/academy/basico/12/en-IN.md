## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A thesis claims every sales delay causes lost deals. A fictional set has 20 opportunities: ten fast responses with four advancing, ten slow responses with three advancing. Fit and industry differ. The offer organises tasks but cannot fix poor fit.

An Indian distributor's slow-response group contains more accounts outside the supported region. A regional supervisor challenges the thesis. The correct response must account for eligibility rather than blame response speed alone.

## Projeto / Project

Build a bounded thesis, calculate rates, give an alternative explanation and define a test capable of refuting the recommendation.

**Resposta-modelo / Model answer**
Observed progress is 40% versus 30%, a 10-percentage-point gap in a small non-comparable sample. It does not prove causality. Fit and source may explain it. Propose comparable criteria and allow a no-benefit outcome.

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.
