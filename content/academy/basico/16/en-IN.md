## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Beacon Group has six sites. Operations wants standard records, IT an access test, procurement vendor onboarding, and finance has not approved funds. The sponsor accepts a presentation but cannot contract. An expansion announcement was incorrectly recorded as approved budget.

An Indian group has regional operational leads and central accounts approval. The requested pilot spans two cities with different staffing availability. The supplier must confirm training support and invoicing requirements without assuming universal rollout.
