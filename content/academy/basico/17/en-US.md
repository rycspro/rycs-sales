## Caso fictício / Fictional case

A team practises visits in an approved fictional territory. One location posts no sales visits, another is serving a customer and a third allows one minute. The offer cannot activate immediately. The rep planned interior photographs without an approved evidence procedure.

A US storefront exercise includes a no-soliciting notice and a manager who asks for a written offer. Local off-premises sales rules and permissions need review; the scenario grants no real-world access permission.

## Projeto / Project

Plan the route, write three openings and endings and produce a visit record without exposing people or documents.

**Resposta-modelo / Model answer**
Respect the notice and access control; do not demand attention during service. In the accepted minute, identify purpose and ask a relevant question. Promise no immediate activation and take no unauthorised photos. Record outcome and preferences.

## Simulação / Role-play

Play the fictional buyer. Reveal supplied facts when asked; say you need to verify unknowns. Do not teach or score during dialogue. After three turns raise a timing or scope concern; in a second round request closure.

Use only the transcript and rubric. Cite evidence, identify gaps and request human review for uncertain scores. Do not issue certificates or approve contracts.

- Identity and clarity: 20 pontos/points
