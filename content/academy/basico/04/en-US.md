## Caso fictício / Fictional case

Appointment Desk received a form requesting WhatsApp information about meeting scheduling. The buyer asks about price and timing, prefers text and supplied no migration inputs. The offer configures two calendars, excluding historical imports. The rep prepared six voice notes and promised tomorrow.

A US maintenance business explicitly chose this messaging channel for the fictional enquiry, but asks that any proposal be emailed. Its systems administrator must verify calendar access; messaging permission must not be inferred for unrelated offers.
